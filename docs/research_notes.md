# Research Notes

Background research supporting design decisions. Reference only.

## Evidence-Based Design Principles

1. **Contemporaneous Documentation** (Kahneman & Tversky)
   - Recall bias after 2-3 weeks distorts 60-80% of event details
   - Justifies: frictionless daily `brag add`

2. **Prompted Structured Reflection** (Boud et al.)
   - Structured prompts increase reflective quality 3x vs free-form
   - Justifies: bucket-specific prompt examples

3. **Progress Visibility & Feedback Loops** (Kivetz et al.)
   - Progress indicators increase task completion 40%
   - Justifies: completeness scores, gamification

4. **Choice Architecture & Defaults** (Thaler & Sunstein)
   - Required confirmations reduce bad data entry
   - Justifies: required field validation for Process/Leadership

5. **Self-Promotion Gap** (Babcock & Laschever)
   - Underrepresented groups under-report achievements 30-50%
   - Justifies: mandatory impact capture as default, not optional

## Competitive Landscape

Features observed in similar tools (SaaS performance platforms, work journals, portfolio builders):

- Auto-import from ticketing systems (Jira, GitHub)
- Peer nomination workflows
- Audience-specific filtering (technical vs business views)
- Contribution heatmaps / visual timelines
- Changelog-style categorization (ADDED/CHANGED/FIXED)
- Impact dashboards with cumulative metrics
